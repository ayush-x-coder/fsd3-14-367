ChatGPT

ChatGPT is an artificial intelligence chatbot developed by OpenAI. It can understand and generate human-like text based on the user's questions and instructions.

ChatGPT can be used for many purposes such as learning, writing, programming, brainstorming, summarizing information, and solving problems. It can explain complex topics in simple language and help users improve their productivity.

ChatGPT is designed to have natural conversations with users. It can answer questions, generate ideas, write code, create documents, and assist with various everyday tasks.

ChatGPT is a useful AI tool for students, developers, professionals, and anyone who wants help with information, creativity, or problem-solving.
