Who We Are

Serving the global game, one member at a time.

We're the international body connecting 211 member associations across six confederations — organising tournaments, setting the rules of the game, and investing in football's future at every level.

Our mission

Football belongs to everyone. Our job is to protect the game's integrity, grow it in every corner of the world, and make sure the sport keeps developing — from a first kickabout to the biggest stage there is.

We govern competitions, set the laws of the game alongside our partners, and channel funding back into member associations so that talent and infrastructure can grow wherever football is played.

Our history

1904

Founded by a handful of national associations to bring order and shared rules to the international game.

1930

The first world championship is held, launching what would become football's biggest tournament.

1991

The inaugural women's world championship expands the global stage to the women's game.

2026

Membership reaches 211 associations, with development funding at an all-time high.
